Why we don't upload

Other tools

Your file travels to their machine, gets processed there and is sent back. Then they promise to delete it “within a few hours.” For a contract, a payslip or an ID scan, you have to take that on trust.

Here

There is no promise to trust. There is no server. Everything happens in your own browser and the file never touches the network — you can verify this yourself by turning off your internet.
